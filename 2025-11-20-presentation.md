# AI Engineering Code Summit 2025-11-20
## Executive Presentation

---

## Slide 1: The Fundamental Shift

**AI isn't replacing software engineering fundamentals—it's amplifying them**

The difference between clean code and technical debt, between documented and tribal knowledge, between good process and chaos is now more consequential than ever before.

*Stanford study: Clean codebases see 35-40% AI productivity gains. Messy codebases: 0-10%*

---

## Slide 2: Trust Through Infrastructure

> "What's good for humans is good for AI"
> — Max Kanat-Alexander, Capital One

**The same investments that help human developers are exactly what AI agents need:**
- Better tooling
- Clearer documentation
- Robust testing

**Proof:** Zapier's support team members achieved 70% accuracy with AI tools and moved into engineering roles, doubling velocity to 3-4 fixes/week

---

## Slide 3: The Harness Is Where You Win

**While models commoditize, competitive advantage lives in the abstraction layer**

OpenAI processes dozens of trillions of tokens/week through their "harness":
- Prompts
- Context management
- Agent loops
- Tool orchestration

**The trust problem:** 76% of developers don't trust AI-generated code because they don't trust the *context* the AI has

---

## Slide 4: Clean Code = 35% Gains vs 0%

**Stanford's 120,000-engineer study reveals a "rich gets richer effect"**

| Codebase Quality | AI Productivity Gains |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| High environment cleanliness | 35-40% |
| Low environment cleanliness | 0-10% |

> "2 engineers can create the tech debt of 50 engineers" — Lei Zhang, Bloomberg

**Environment cleanliness predicts AI productivity far better than token usage**

---

## Slide 5: ROI Reality - Greenfield ≠ Brownfield

**Where AI delivers:**
- Greenfield projects: 35-40% gains
- Low-complexity tasks: clear ROI

**Where AI struggles:**
- Brownfield codebases: 0-10% gains
- High-complexity work: minimal impact

**93% of organizations stuck in "pilot purgatory"** — only 7% believe they're at scale (McKinsey)

---

## Slide 6: The Demo-to-Production Gap

**Northwestern Mutual's 4 barriers in risk-averse environments:**
1. Unknown technology
2. Messy real data
3. Blind-trust bias
4. Budget impact concerns

**The solution:**
- Stop using synthetic data
- Bring business stakeholders into research from day one
- Build trust incrementally with real messy data
- Deliver ROI step by step

---

## Slide 7: From Reactive to Proactive Agents

**Current tools:** "Fundamentally reactive" — forcing constant context-switching like "reminding the husband to do the dishes"

**Proactive agents (Google's Jules):**
- Observation: understand what you're working on
- Timely action: know when to step in
- Personalization: learn what you ignore
- Seamless integration: reduce mental load

**Goal:** Remove overhead of coordination, context management, task sequencing

---

## Slide 8: Economics Are Breaking

**When AI can generate a developer's annual salary in tokens for $100/day:**

Traditional time-based compensation becomes obsolete

**Tenex's response:** Pay engineers like salespeople
- Output-based, uncapped compensation
- Multiple engineers on track for $1M+ annually
- "Give your team a reason to go faster"

**Gene Kim:** When costs drop 100x, "annoying things become free"

---

## Slide 9: Role Boundaries Are Dissolving

**Every: 15 people, 6 business units, 7-figure revenue**
- Managers commit code
- New hires productive day one
- Engineers work with "fractured attention" productively

**The dividing line isn't skill level—it's adaptability**

> "We spend more time reading code than writing it, and even more so now. Every software developer becomes a code reviewer." — Max Kanat-Alexander

---

## Slide 10: Harness Architecture Patterns

**Replit:** Subagents with fresh context prevent "context pollution"
- Core loop as orchestrator
- 30%+ of user features were broken — autonomous testing breaks bottleneck

**Anthropic:** Memory + Context Editing
- Retrieves relevant context when needed
- Manually clears unnecessary content

**Browser Company:** Prompt editors in dev tools → 10x iteration speed

**"Steerability = intelligence + habit"** — OpenAI

---

## Slide 11: What To Do - Infrastructure First

**1. Invest in code cleanliness before scaling AI adoption**
- Refactor for testability
- Improve deterministic validation
- Write down tribal knowledge
- This is the 35-40% vs 0-10% difference

**2. Build your harness as strategic IP**
- Context management
- Subagent architecture
- Memory systems
- Models commoditize; your abstraction layer won't

---

## Slide 12: What To Do - Start Smart

**3. Start with unglamorous, high-ROI tasks**
- Focus on work developers hate
- Incident response, migrations, uplift agents
- Bloomberg saw usage drop on brownfield; pivoted to clear-value tasks

**4. Use real messy data from day one**
- Stop building with synthetic data
- Bring stakeholders into research projects
- Build trust incrementally, not big-bang

---

## Slide 13: What To Do - Break Bottlenecks

**5. Build autonomous testing to break the review bottleneck**

> "You have more bugs because there are more quantity of PRs, not because the PRs themselves are more buggy." — Itamar Friedman, Qodo

The constraint is review velocity

**6. Rethink compensation models**
- If AI can 10x productivity, align incentives
- Output-based models match the new reality

---

## Slide 14: The Bottom Line

**AI rewards excellence and punishes mediocrity**

Organizations racing to adopt every new model are missing the point.

**Those winning are methodically fixing foundational problems:**
- Clean code → testability → documentation → deterministic validation
- The same problems that have plagued development for decades

**"What's good for humans is good for AI" is a north star for enterprise AI strategy**

---

## Slide 15: Questions?

**Key Insight:**

Stop building for the agents.

Start building systems that work for everyone.
