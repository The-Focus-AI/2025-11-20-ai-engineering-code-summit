## About Northwestern Mutual
Northwestern Mutual is a Fortune 100 financial services company providing life insurance, disability insurance, annuities, and advisory services. The company is known for its conservative, risk-averse approach while managing substantial assets and data. Northwestern Mutual has invested significantly in AI and data science initiatives, including the Northwestern Mutual Data Science Institute, to balance innovation with their traditional business model of long-term customer relationships spanning 40-50 years.

## Speaker Background
Asaf Bord is a product leader with over 19 years of technology experience, with 12+ years specializing in product management. He holds both an M.Sc. in Bio-Medical Engineering (specializing in computer vision and medical imaging) and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Technion - Israel Institute of Technology. ## Research Notes
Asaf Bord leads Northwestern Mutual's GenBI (Generative Business Intelligence) initiative, applying a "crawl-walk-run" approach to enterprise AI adoption. His session focused on overcoming four key barriers to GenAI in conservative financial services organizations: unknown technology, messy real data, trust bias, and budget/impact validation. Key themes from his work include:

**Implementation Strategy:**
- Using actual production data instead of synthetic data to understand real-world complexity
- Bridging the gap between demo and production environments
- Building business stakeholder participation directly into the research process
- Creating incremental value delivery and ROI at each step to build organizational trust

**Technical Approach:**
- Business question to orchestrator pattern for query processing
- Metadata agents for understanding context and lineage
- RAG agents connected to certified existing reports
- Schema and lookup optimization for scalability

**Organizational Challenges:**
- Managing risk-averse culture while driving innovation
- Balancing long-term customer relationships with technology advancement
- Pricing decisions in the new era of AI-powered software (usage-based vs. seat-based models)

Asaf has published extensively on GenBI through his Substack on product, strategy, and leadership topics. He's also a Content Manager for Product Excellence, the largest PM conference in Israel with 1,200+ annual attendees. His work demonstrates deep expertise in implementing AI solutions within highly regulated, conservative enterprises where building trust and demonstrating incremental value are critical to success.
