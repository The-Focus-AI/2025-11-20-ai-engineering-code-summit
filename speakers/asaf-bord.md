GenAI Products Leader at Northwestern Mutual, pioneering enterprise generative business intelligence (GenBI) at a Fortune 100 financial services company with a "crawl-walk-run" approach to AI adoption in conservative environments.

## Current Role & Co-Founder

Leads Northwestern Mutual's GenBI initiative, applying AI to enterprise data intelligence in a highly regulated environment. Co-founder of [Multinear](https://multinear.com), an open-source platform for tracking AI experiments and managing models from prototype to production.

Key focus areas:
- Business question to orchestrator pattern for query processing
- Metadata agents for understanding context and lineage
- RAG agents connected to certified existing reports
- Building stakeholder participation directly into research

Content Manager for Product Excellence, Israel's largest PM conference (1,200+ annual attendees).

## Conference Appearance

**AI Engineering Code Summit 2025**
**November 20, 2025 | 2:05 PM - 2:24 PM**
**Session:** "From Small Bets to Big Impact: Building GenBI at a Fortune 100"

Asaf presented on overcoming four key barriers to GenAI in conservative financial services: unknown technology, messy real data, trust bias, and budget/impact validation. His approach emphasizes using actual production data instead of synthetic data, bridging the demo-to-production gap, and building incremental ROI at each step to establish organizational trust in risk-averse environments.
