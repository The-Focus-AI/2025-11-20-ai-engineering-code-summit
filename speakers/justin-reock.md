## Systems Thinking Meets AI Strategy

Justin Reock has become a leading voice in AI engineering adoption, advocating for systems-level thinking over individual productivity metrics. His philosophy: treat software development as a systems problem, measure what matters, and build psychological safety to accelerate AI transformation.

## Philosophy on AI Adoption

Justin's approach emphasizes culture and measurement as much as tooling:

**Find the bottleneck, fix the bottleneck** - Strategic AI implementation should target specific constraints, like Zapier's approach that unblocked developers and enabled hiring growth.

**Psychological safety drives adoption** - References Google's Project Aristotle research showing psychological safety as the overwhelming predictor of team effectiveness. Reduce fear through education and transparency.

**Systems thinking over individual metrics** - Integrate AI across the entire SDLC, not just coding. Look at development as a system, identify constraints, and optimize holistically.

**Never use temperature extremes** - Avoid 0 or 1; use values like 0.00001 for more deterministic behavior while maintaining model flexibility.

**Implement feedback loops** - Continuous improvement requires mechanisms for evaluating and refining system prompts and AI configurations.
