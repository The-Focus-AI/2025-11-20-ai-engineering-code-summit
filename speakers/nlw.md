## Philosophy on AI Adoption

**Data over hype** - Questions dubious claims and focuses on what organizations are actually achieving with measurable outcomes

**The adoption gap** - While KPMG surveys show agent use jumping from 11% (Q1) to 42% (Q3), only 7% of organizations believe they're truly at scale. Most remain stuck in "pilot purgatory"

**Jagged adoption patterns** - IT and Ops departments jumping ahead of other use cases, with significant differences between leading organizations and laggards
