## Key Research & Insights

### Enterprise Adoption Status (2025)

NLW's research reveals critical insights into where enterprise AI adoption actually stands:

**The Adoption Gap:**
- KPMG agent use survey shows dramatic increase: 11% (Q1) to 42% (Q3)
- Decreased resistance to AI agents deployment
- **However:** Many organizations stuck in pilots and experimental phases
  - Only 7% believe they are fully at scale
  - Jagged patterns of adoption across departments
  - IT and Ops jumping ahead of other use cases

**Leadership vs. Laggards:**
- Big differences between leading organizations and those lagging in deployment
- AI spending increasing dramatically year-over-year
- Growing optimism about deployment throughout 2025

### ROI Study Findings

Conducted research analyzing 3,500+ AI use cases across 8 impact categories:

**Key Findings:**
- Couple hours a week of AI usage translates to multiple work weeks of value annually
- Different company sizes see different impact patterns
- Different roles experience different benefits from same tools
- **Coding is further ahead** in adoption and ROI realization compared to other domains

**Impact Categories:**
The research identified 8 distinct impact areas where AI delivers measurable ROI, with coding/development being the most mature.

## Key Vision & Philosophy

### From Hype to Reality

NLW's core contribution is grounding AI adoption discussions in actual data and real-world outcomes. Key principles:

- **Data-driven perspective:** Question dubious claims (e.g., early MIT reports) and focus on what organizations are actually achieving
- **Pattern recognition:** Identify where AI is delivering ROI vs. where it's stuck in experiments
- **Honest assessment:** Acknowledge that many organizations remain in "pilot purgatory" despite high adoption enthusiasm
- **Practical consulting:** Share patterns from real-world implementations that others can replicate

### The Adoption Challenge

While enterprise interest in AI agents is high, the reality is more complex:

- Enterprises excited about potential but struggling with scale
- Many projects stuck in pilots without moving to production
- ROI measured inconsistently across organizations
- Successful adoption requires both technical capability and organizational readiness
