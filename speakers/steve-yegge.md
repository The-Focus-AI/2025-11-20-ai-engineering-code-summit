## Philosophy on AI-Driven Development

Steve's contrarian vision challenges the software engineering status quo:

**Traditional IDEs are obsolete** - "If you are still using an IDE by Jan 1st you are a bad engineer." AI agents represent the future.

**Models are ready** - The bottleneck isn't model capability; it's tooling and human adaptation. Current models are "smart enough, strictly."

**Ants vs. divers** - Many specialized agents beat one powerful agent. Context windows are like oxygen tanks - use them strategically.

**10x productivity is real** - Enterprise case studies at Traveloppia, Cisco, Capital One, and Fidelity prove dramatic efficiency gains.

**Trust through practice** - AI trust increases with experience, building Fingerspitzengefuhl (intuitive feel) through repeated use.
