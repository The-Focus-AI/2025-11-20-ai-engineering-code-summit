## Champion of Flexible AI Monetization

Mike Lacsamana addresses a critical challenge often overlooked in AI engineering: how to sustainably monetize AI services while maintaining product flexibility and user satisfaction. His work focuses on building billing infrastructure that adapts to the unique economics of AI products.

## Philosophy on AI Billing

Mike's approach emphasizes practical solutions to AI-specific challenges:

**Transparency builds trust** - Users demand visibility into token consumption and costs; opaque billing drives churn while clear cost breakdowns enable informed usage decisions.

**Flexibility is essential** - AI economics change rapidly, requiring billing systems that adapt quickly without engineering bottlenecks or costly migrations.

**Credits enable growth** - Beyond monetization, credit systems serve as powerful tools for user retention, feature experimentation, and churn reduction through unspent credit re-engagement.

**Infrastructure first** - The right billing foundation enables faster product iteration and sustainable unit economics as AI costs fluctuate.
