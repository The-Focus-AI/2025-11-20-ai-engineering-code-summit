## The Challenge: Billing for AI Products

Traditional billing systems were designed for:
- Predictable, linear SaaS usage patterns
- Simple seat-based or feature-based pricing
- Relatively stable cost structures

AI products present unique challenges:
- **Variable Token Consumption:** LLM costs vary wildly based on model, task complexity, and user behavior
- **Unpredictable Economics:** Cost per user isn't fixed; it depends on usage patterns
- **Dynamic Pricing Requirements:** Companies need to adjust pricing rapidly based on costs and market
- **Credit Management Complexity:** Managing pre-paid credits, refunds, and subscription combinations
- **Transparency Demands:** Users want to understand exactly what they're paying for

## Key Insights on AI Billing

### The Cost Problem
- **Transparent Pricing:** Users increasingly demand visibility into token consumption and costs
- **Cost Absorption:** Companies must balance markup over API costs with competitive positioning
- **Sustainable Models:** Long-term profitability requires accurate cost tracking and allocation

### Credit Systems as a Strategic Tool
- **User Retention:** Credit systems can smooth revenue and improve unit economics
- **Experimentation:** Credits allow users to try features without commitment
- **Flexibility:** Enables bundled packages, promotions, and personalized pricing
- **Churn Reduction:** Credit balance acts as a retention mechanism

### Scaling Considerations
- **Micro-transactions:** AI APIs enable pay-per-use at unprecedented scale
- **Real-time Accounting:** Billing must account for costs in near real-time
- **Multi-model Economics:** Supporting multiple models with different cost structures
- **Geographic Pricing:** Costs vary by region; billing should reflect this

## Key Concepts

### 1. Usage-Based Billing for AI
Unlike traditional SaaS:
- Pricing is tied directly to token consumption or API calls
- Costs are variable and partially passed through to customers
- Requires real-time billing rather than monthly reconciliation

### 2. Credit Systems Strategy
Credits serve multiple purposes:
- **Monetization:** Pre-paid credits generate revenue upfront
- **Product Experimentation:** Users explore features with "free" credits
- **Churn Prevention:** Unspent credits encourage re-engagement
- **Complexity Management:** Bundles hide complexity while improving margins

### 3. Financial Architecture Patterns
- **Tiered Consumption Models:** Different price points for different user segments
- **Commitment-Based Discounts:** Annual pre-payment options to improve cash flow
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Adjusting prices based on demand, costs, and competition
- **Blended Pricing:** Combining fixed fees with variable usage charges

## Stigg's Solution

Stigg addresses the AI billing gap by providing:

- **User Transparency:** Customers see exactly what they're using with out-of-the-box balance and utilization widgets
- **No Migration Required:** Credits Suite works on top of existing billing infrastructure

## Key Takeaways

1. **Billing is Product:** How you charge isn't just financial engineering—it's a core product decision
