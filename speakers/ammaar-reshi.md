## Philosophy on AI Development

**Accessibility over expertise** - Breaking down barriers so anyone can build software without years of programming experience, embodying the thesis that this is the first generation where anyone can create applications.

**Design sensibility over mechanics** - Focus on what you want to build; AI handles the how. Demonstrated through live demos building comic books, interactive games, and full-stack applications.

**Modern aesthetic standards** - Advocating for contemporary design patterns, shader animations, and professional interfaces that reflect current best practices, moving away from outdated "cyberpunk purple gradients."

## About Google AI Studio

Google AI Studio is a free platform providing access to Gemini models with integrated tools including API key management, Google Search grounding for real-time information, multiple AI capabilities, and one-click app deployment. The platform embodies recursive AI development - AI Studio was used to build AI Studio itself.
