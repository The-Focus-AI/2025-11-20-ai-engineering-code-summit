## Overview

Ammaar Reshi is a Product & Design Lead at Google DeepMind, focused on making AI accessible to everyone by democratizing software creation. He co-presented alongside Kat Kampf on the power of Gemini 3 Pro and AI Studio, showcasing how the latest generation of AI models enables non-traditional developers to build production-quality software without traditional coding expertise.

## Background & Role

### Google DeepMind Position

Ammaar works as a **Product & Design Lead** at Google DeepMind, where he focuses on:

- **Democratizing software creation** - Making it possible for anyone to build software
- **Product experience design** - Creating intuitive interfaces for AI-powered development
- **Creative applications** - Exploring novel use cases for frontier AI models
- **User-centered AI** - Designing with developers and creators in mind

## Key Focus Areas

### 1. Gemini 3 Pro Capabilities

Ammaar highlighted Gemini 3 Pro as a transformative model that enables:

- **UI and design sensibility** - Modern, thoughtful interfaces without cyberpunk aesthetics
- **Agentic tool calling** - Models that can autonomously use tools and APIs
- **Creative expression** - Building compelling user experiences with AI assistance
- **Search integration** - Using Google Search grounding to provide accurate, current information

### 2. AI Studio & The Build Experience

Presented **AI Studio/Build** as a free platform enabling:

## Key Philosophy

### Democratization of Software

Ammaar's core belief: **"First generation where anyone can build software"**

This reflects a fundamental shift in software development:

- **Removing barriers** - No need for years of programming experience
- **Accessibility** - Entrepreneurs, designers, and creators can build
- **Speed to production** - From idea to working application rapidly
- **Quality standards** - Professional-grade output without professional backgrounds

### Design Sensibility Over Mechanics

- **Focus on the vision** - What do you want to build?
- **AI handles mechanics** - The how is managed by the model
- **User experience first** - Design decisions guide implementation
- **Creative freedom** - Less constrained by technical limitations
