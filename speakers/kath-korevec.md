Director of Product at Google Labs leading AI Developer Assistants (AIDA). Previously VP of Product at Vercel, Senior Director at GitHub, and product leader at Heroku. Over a decade of experience building developer tools and platforms that developers love.

## Champion of Proactive, Ambient AI Agents

Kath Korevec has emerged as a leading voice advocating for the shift from reactive chat-based AI to proactive, ambient agents that anticipate developer needs. Her philosophy: "The patterns of how we use an IDE right now might not exist at all next year."

## Current Work: Google Labs AIDA

At Google Labs, Kath leads the AI Developer Assistants team building next-generation autonomous coding tools:

- **Jules** - Autonomous coding agent powered by Gemini 2.5 Pro, handling async tasks from bug fixes to performance optimization. Now available via [jules.google](https://jules.google) with CLI tools and API access.
- **Stitch** - AI-powered app design tool at [stitchai.org](https://stitchai.org/) for rapidly creating UI from text or image prompts.

Her team focuses on proactive agents that observe context, take timely action, personalize to individual developers, and integrate seamlessly into existing workflows.

## Philosophy: The Mental Load Problem

Kath frames AI agents through a relatable metaphor: just as someone needs to remind their husband to do the dishes, developers face significant mental load managing repetitive tasks. Her vision centers on four pillars:

1. **Observation** - Understanding development context continuously
2. **Timely Action** - Acting proactively at the right moment
3. **Personalization** - Learning individual developer patterns
4. **Seamless Integration** - Embedding into workflows without friction

Key insight: When compute becomes unlimited, agents should handle mechanical work so humans can focus on creativity and architecture.
