## Building Radically AI-Native Companies

Dan Shipper has built Every as a living proof of concept: a company where engineers write virtually zero code by hand, where AI agents ship features in parallel, and where 15 people accomplish what traditionally requires 50+. His work demonstrates that AI isn't just a productivity tool—it's a fundamental reimagining of how knowledge companies operate.

## Philosophy: Compounding Engineering

Dan articulates a paradigm shift from traditional software development:

**Traditional Engineering** accumulates technical debt—each feature makes the codebase harder to work with. **Compounding Engineering** leverages AI to create the opposite dynamic—each feature makes the next one easier because AI learns from prior implementations. Knowledge compounds instead of debt.

This enables counterintuitive outcomes: fractured attention becomes viable, managers can commit code, and new hires are productive on day one. The secret is codifying tacit knowledge into prompts that capture organizational standards and practices.
