## Empowering Support Teams to Ship Code

Lisa Orr leads one of the most compelling organizational experiments in AI-assisted development: enabling support teams to write and ship production code. At Zapier, she's developed Scout MCP (built on Cursor and Model Context Protocol) that lets support specialists diagnose issues, generate fixes, and validate solutions—achieving 70% accuracy rates and doubling team velocity to 3-4 fixes per week.

## Philosophy on AI-Assisted Development

Lisa's approach challenges conventional organizational boundaries:

**Support teams are uniquely positioned for AI-assisted coding** - They're closest to customer pain, excel at real-time troubleshooting, and best understand whether fixes actually solve user problems.

**Orchestration over isolation** - The true value emerges from tiering tools together. Scout MCP combines Cursor's AI capabilities with Zapier's automation platform, not using them in isolation.

**Role elevation, not replacement** - Rather than automating support away, empower support specialists to implement solutions directly. This creates natural career pathways from support into engineering, bypassing traditional academic gatekeeping.
