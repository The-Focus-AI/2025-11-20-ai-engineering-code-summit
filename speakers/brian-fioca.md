## About OpenAI
OpenAI is an AI research company that creates advanced language models and AI systems. The company is known for developing GPT models, including the latest GPT-5, and providing tools like Codex for coding tasks. OpenAI provides APIs and solutions for building AI-powered applications across various domains.

## Speaker Background
Brian Fioca is a serial entrepreneur and product engineer with over 20 years of experience building software products. He is the co-founder of RescueTime (Y Combinator Winter 2008), a leading time management and productivity tool. At OpenAI, he works as a Solutions Architect helping startups leverage OpenAI's APIs and models for innovative applications. Prior to joining OpenAI in May 2024, Fioca was an AI engineer at Pioneer Square Labs and has co-created successful ventures at Madrona Venture Labs, including Mighty AI (acquired by Uber) and MessageYes (acquired by Nordstrom).

## Research Notes
Brian Fioca joined OpenAI in May 2024 and has been instrumental in showcasing the company's latest agent and voice technologies. His talk on "Future-Proof Coding Agents" focuses on building coding agents that remain reliable as models evolve, emphasizing the importance of the "harness" (prompts, agent loops, and tool descriptions) rather than just the underlying model. He stresses that with rapid model development, the key to sustainable agent systems is designing flexible architectures that can adapt to new models without requiring complete rewrites. His expertise spans from early-stage startup innovation to large-scale AI infrastructure, making him well-positioned to advise both startups and enterprises on AI adoption strategies. Fioca was featured in OpenAI's GPT-5 livestream announcement in August 2025 and continues to be active in demonstrating production-grade agent systems and voice technologies.
