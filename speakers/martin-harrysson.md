# Martin Harrysson

## Overview

Martin Harrysson is a Partner at McKinsey & Company, a global management consulting firm. He is a leading expert on organizational transformation in the age of AI, with a particular focus on how software development practices and team structures must evolve to leverage AI-driven development methodologies. Martin co-presented the session "Moving away from Agile: What's Next?" at the AI Engineering Code Summit.

## Professional Background

- **Position**: Partner, McKinsey & Company
- **Focus Areas**:

  - Post-Agile methodologies and frameworks
  - Organizational transformation and change management
  - AI-native development practices
  - Software development lifecycle (PDLC) modernization
  - Team structure optimization in AI-augmented environments

## McKinsey Research & Publications

### Flagship Studies

#### "Unlocking the Value of AI in Software Development"
McKinsey's comprehensive study examining how organizations can effectively adopt AI in software development practices. This research informs Martin's work on:
- Impact of AI on development roles and responsibilities
- Team reorganization strategies around AI agents
- Change management approaches for large-scale transformation
- Greenfield and brownfield development strategies

#### 120,000 Developer Study
McKinsey conducted extensive research across 120,000 developers to understand:
- Current state of software development practices
- Adoption patterns of AI tools and methodologies
- Impact on productivity and team performance
- Gap between current Agile practices and AI-native approaches
- Role transformation and skill requirements

### Research Themes

Martin's research covers critical topics including:
- **Beyond Agile**: Moving from traditional Agile methodologies toward AI-native development frameworks ("Software X")
- **Team Transformation**: Evolution from 10x individual engineers to 10x high-performing teams augmented by AI
- **AI-Native Roles**: New types of development roles that emerge in AI-augmented environments
- **Specification-Driven Development**: Shift from story-driven development to specification/PRD-driven approaches with AI generating code from long-form documentation
- **Agent Managers**: New leadership roles focused on managing and coordinating AI agents rather than supervising individual developers
- **Change Management**: Practical strategies for organizational adoption of AI development practices

## Speaking Topics & Summit Presentation

### "Moving away from Agile: What's Next?" (November 20, 2025)

Co-presented with Natasha Maniar (McKinsey Consultant/Analyst), this keynote session explored:

**Key Themes:**
1. **The Agile Paradigm Shift**: Why traditional Agile methodologies need re-examination in the context of AI-driven development
2. **Software X Framework**: A new development paradigm moving beyond Agile to accommodate AI capabilities
3. **Organizational Bottlenecks**: Identifying and solving critical bottlenecks in task allocation and team coordination
4. **AI-Native Development**: How specification-driven development differs from traditional story-driven approaches
5. **Code Generation from PDFs**: Leveraging long-form PRDs to generate codebases with AI agents
6. **Agent-Centric Management**: Managing teams of AI agents rather than individual developers
7. **Practical Implementation**: Small interventions and change cycles that drive meaningful transformation
8. **Sprint and Team Restructuring**: Moving toward shorter sprints, smaller team sizes, and more numerous teams

**Insights Shared:**
- The entire software development framework requires modernization
- Cursor's internal operations provide insights into effective AI-augmented development
- Role transformation hasn't fully occurred yet in most organizations—significant opportunity ahead
- Change management requires getting "a lot of little things right"
- Successful transformation involves multiple small interventions and iterative change cycles
- Investment in greenfield and brownfield development is increasing
- Organizations should start transformation initiatives now—cultural and human change takes time

## Key Concepts & Philosophy

Martin Harrysson's work emphasizes:

1. **Paradigm Shift**: The entire software development lifecycle (PDLC) needs rewriting, not incremental improvement
2. **Team Augmentation**: Moving from optimizing individual developer productivity to optimizing AI-augmented team productivity
3. **Specification-Centric**: Shifting from user story-driven development to specification/PRD-driven development with AI code generation
4. **Agent Management**: New management competencies focused on coordinating AI agents and human oversight
5. **Incremental Change**: Successful transformation involves many small, well-executed interventions rather than one large initiative
6. **Human-Centered Transformation**: Technology change is secondary to organizational and human change—takes significant time and effort

## Summit Session Context

At the AI Engineering Code Summit (November 20, 2025), Martin Harrysson presented one of the most transformational sessions, challenging conventional wisdom about Agile methodologies and proposing frameworks for the next generation of software development. His presentation at 11:20 AM (with co-presenter Natasha Maniar) positioned McKinsey's research as foundational to understanding how the industry must evolve beyond traditional practices.

The timing of this session alongside other presentations on AI coding agents (Steve Yegge on Cursor/Amp, Gene Kim on IDE transformation) created a coherent narrative about how development practices, tools, and organizational structures are all fundamentally changing together.

## Key Insights & Takeaways

1. **Agile is Not Dead, But Evolving**: The principles of Agile remain relevant, but the implementation needs radical redesign
2. **Start with Specification**: Long-form PRDs and specifications become the primary development input, with AI generating implementations
3. **Team Reorganization**: Flatten and distribute teams across more numerous, smaller units with agent-manager oversight
4. **Change Now**: Organizations delaying transformation will fall behind—the change is human and cultural, requiring immediate action
5. **Small Wins Matter**: Successful transformation happens through accumulation of small, well-executed changes rather than single large initiatives
6. **Role Transformation**: Developer roles, manager roles, and organizational structures must all evolve to leverage AI effectively
